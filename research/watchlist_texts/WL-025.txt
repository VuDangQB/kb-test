# Masan Consumer: Double-digit growth, aiming for 20-25% annual shareholder return.

Masan Consumer (HOSE: MCH) aims to increase long-term shareholder value with a target of 20-25% per year, based on business growth and cash dividends. With preliminary revenue for Q3/2026 increasing by approximately 16% and the company planning to allocate up to 80% of after-tax profits to dividend payments, what are the driving forces behind MCH's achievement of this expectation?
Where do the 20-25% annual shareholder returns come from?
Masan Consumer (HOSE: MCH) projected preliminary revenue for Q3 2026 to increase by approximately 16% year-on-year. Along with plans to expand its market, develop new product lines, and allocate up to 80% of its after-tax profit to cash dividends, the company aims to increase long-term shareholder value through both business growth and dividends.
In its 2026 investor relations document, Masan Consumer outlines a long-term shareholder return of 20-25% annually. This figure is derived from two main sources of benefit: potential enterprise value appreciation as profits grow and cash dividends received by shareholders.
Specifically, Masan Consumer expects profits from existing product lines to increase by an average of 10-15% annually. Expansion into beverages, international markets, and new product lines is expected to contribute an additional 5% growth. Furthermore, the company assumes a cash dividend yield of approximately 5% per year, resulting in a projected total shareholder return of 20-25% per year.
In other words, Masan Consumer aims to both develop its business operations to increase enterprise value and share profits with shareholders through dividends. This is a long-term strategy based on growth assumptions, not a commitment to a fixed rate of return or annual stock price increase.
Double-digit growth, expanding momentum from new markets.
Recent business results show that Masan Consumer is maintaining positive growth momentum. According to preliminary figures for Q3/2026, the company's revenue increased by approximately 16% year-on-year, while operating profit also recorded double-digit growth.
Previously, in the first half of 2026, Masan Consumer achieved revenue of VND 15,637 billion, an increase of 13.6%; after-tax profit reached approximately VND 3,284 billion, an increase of 10.9% compared to the same period.

The growth was supported by key brands and the expansion of the sales network. In Q2 2026, CHIN-SU's revenue increased by approximately 30%, Omachi by 14.3%, while the personal and household care product group increased by 59.6%.
Through the Retail Supreme platform, Masan Consumer is expanding its distribution network and increasing the number of products at each point of sale. By Q2/2026, the company aims to have approximately 550,000 operating points of sale nationwide. Adding spices, convenience foods, beverages, and household care products to the network is expected to boost sales at existing stores while bringing new products closer to consumers.
Besides the domestic market, Masan Consumer is seeking growth opportunities in beverages, higher-value products, and international markets. According to investor documents, the company aims to increase the proportion of international revenue from approximately 5% in 2025 to 15–20% by 2030.

Brands like CHIN-SU are expected to reach more consumers in the US and Asian markets, while the beverage and personal/household care sectors continue to grow to meet the increasingly diverse needs of consumers.
The expansion into new business areas is expected to generate additional growth alongside traditional product lines, thereby supporting the goal of increasing profitability in the coming years.
Cash flow and dividends: Sharing the fruits of labor with shareholders.
Besides profit growth, cash dividends are an important part of Masan Consumer's long-term strategy.
According to a report by TCBS, Masan Consumer has paid out approximately VND 46,000 billion in cash dividends to shareholders during the period 2017-2025. In August 2026 alone, the company made its first interim dividend payment of the year at VND 2,000 per share, equivalent to a total of approximately VND 2,600 billion.
Notably, according to information released on October 6, 2026, Masan Consumer plans to allocate up to 80% of its after-tax profit to cash dividends. This means that for every 100 VND of after-tax profit generated, the company expects to be able to distribute up to 80 VND to shareholders. The actual payout amount will depend on business results and company decisions.

The company's ability to generate cash from business operations also improved in the first half of 2026. According to the TCBS report, cash flow from business operations reached VND 2,243 billion, compared to VND 80 billion in the same period last year. After deducting investment expenses, the company had approximately VND 1,730 billion in free cash flow, higher than the level recorded for the whole year of 2025.
This improvement shows that Masan Consumer not only increased profits but also generated cash flow from its business operations. This is a crucial factor that provides the company with additional resources to develop products, expand its market, and implement its dividend plan.
For long-term shareholders, Masan Consumer's value therefore comes not only from business results in a few quarters, but also from its ability to maintain growth and share profits over many years. Although purchasing power, competition, and raw material costs may still affect actual results, leveraging brand strength, expanding markets, and maintaining cash flow will be crucial factors for the company to gradually realize its announced long-term strategy.
